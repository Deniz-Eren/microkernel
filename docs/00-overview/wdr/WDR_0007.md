# WDR_0007 — Align clang-format, clang-tidy, and Source-Text Configs

```{wdr} Align clang-format, clang-tidy, and source-text configs
:id: WDR_0007
:status: accepted
:governs: ARC_DOCS_0001

## Context

Static analysis flagged intentional linker symbols (`__bss_start`,
`__text_start`) as reserved identifiers and flagged syscall out-parameters as
uninitialized. Also, `.clang-format` wrapped at 100 columns while
`.editorconfig` and `check_text.py` used 80 columns. Verbatim third-party
specifications also caused incorrect text-check errors.

## Decision

- Turn off `bugprone-reserved-identifier` in `.clang-tidy` and export
  `build/compile_commands.json` in `scripts/env.sh`. Suppress incorrect syscall
  out-parameter errors at call sites with named `NOLINT` comments or 0
  initialization.
- Set `ColumnLimit: 80` in `.clang-format` to agree with `.editorconfig`.
- Do not include immutable verbatim documents (`LICENSE.md` and
  `docs/skills/*/references/*`) in `.editorconfig` checks, and add Doxygen
  headers to human-authored sources.

## Rejected alternatives

- Removal of `clang-analyzer-core.*` checks across the full project.
- Increase of `.editorconfig` line limits to 100 columns for C files.

## Results

- `clang-format`, `clang-tidy`, `.editorconfig`, and `check_text.py --root .`
  agree on 80 columns and succeed with no errors across the repository.
- Immutable specification documents stay verbatim and unchanged.
```
