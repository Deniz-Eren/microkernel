# WDR_0006 — Enforce ASD-STE100 Issue 9 and Modal Verb must

```{wdr} Enforce ASD-STE100 Issue 9 and modal verb must
:id: WDR_0006
:status: accepted
:governs: ARC_DOCS_0001

## Context

Project documents need clear, consistent, machine-checkable technical English.
Earlier, `.docs-traceability-setup.yml` used `authoring.modal_verb: shall`,
which violates ASD-STE100 Issue 9 (only `can`, `cannot`, `must`, and `will` are
approved modals).

## Decision

Write `README.md` and all non-immutable documents in `docs/` (including
Sphinx-Needs `{sys}`, `{swr}`, and specification directives) in ASD-STE100 Issue
9. Set `authoring.modal_verb: must` in `.docs-traceability-setup.yml`, keep
sentences within 25 words, and maintain project terms in `technical_terms.txt`
and `allowed_phrases.txt`.

## Rejected alternatives

- Writing free prose with no vocabulary checks.
- Keeping `shall` in requirements and excluding `docs/20-system/` from STE
  checks.

## Consequences

- Governance documents, specifications, and requirements follow one controlled
  language verified by `tools/check_ste.py`.
- Writers add new domain terms to `technical_terms.txt` or `allowed_phrases.txt`
  when they introduce new subsystems.
```
