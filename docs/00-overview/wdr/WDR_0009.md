# WDR_0009 — Move Previous Template Documents into Sphinx-Needs

```{wdr} Move previous template documents into Sphinx-Needs
:id: WDR_0009
:status: accepted
:governs: ARC_DOCS_0001, ADR_0001

## Context

When `ADR_0001` added Sphinx-Needs, five previous `agent-scaffolding` documents
(`docs/TASKS.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/CONVENTIONS.md`, `docs/VISION.md`,
and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`) stayed omitted through `model.code_tag_exclude`.
That made two parallel document trees that became different across commits.

## Decision

Move all unique milestones, decisions, conventions, vision principles, and
architecture items from those five files into the Sphinx-Needs model
(`docs/records/`, `docs/00-overview/`, `docs/10-product/`, and
`docs/30-architecture/adr/`). Remove the five previous files and remove them
from `code_tag_exclude` in `.docs-traceability-setup.yml`. Point `AGENTS.md`,
`README.md`, and `docs/skills/` to the Sphinx-Needs documents. Keep
`docs/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` because no traceability document replaces legal
third-party attributions.

## Rejected alternatives

- Two sets of Markdown files (`agent-scaffolding` and Sphinx-Needs) kept in
  agreement by hand on each commit.
- Removal of previous files without copies of `ADR-1` through `ADR-19`,
  Milestones 1 to 4, or file-name conventions.

## Results

- The repository keeps one source of truth for tasks, history, roadmap,
  conventions, vision, context, and decisions (`ADR_`, `BDR_`, `WDR_`).
- All agent workflows and pre-commit checks operate directly on the traceable
  Sphinx-Needs model, and `docs/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` stays unchanged.
```
