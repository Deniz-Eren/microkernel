# ADR_0007 — Dynamic PMM Refcounts, VMM_COW Faults, and Milestone 5 ABI

```{adr} Dynamic PMM refcounts, VMM_COW faults, and Milestone 5 ABI
:id: ADR_0007
:status: accepted

## Context

Milestone 5 (`SWR_MEM_0004`, `SWR_PROC_0007`) requires `ProcCreate`, `VMMap`,
`VMUnmap`, `VMCloneCow`, and child exit-code collection for POSIX `<sys/mman.h>`
and `<sys/wait.h>`. Copy-on-write frame sharing needs safe lifetime tracking and
Ring 3 `#PF` resolution without kernel `.bss` bloat or W^X violations.

## Decision

- Allocate a per-frame `uint16_t` reference-count table dynamically at the end
  of `pmm_init()` via `pmm_alloc_contiguous()` (~128 KiB on 256 MiB RAM), and
  make `pmm_free_frame()` refcount-aware with `pmm_frame_inc()` and
  `pmm_frame_refcount()`.
- Reserve x86-64 software PTE bit 9 as `VMM_COW`. Resolve COW faults in
  `vmm_resolve_cow_to()` from `idt.c` (`#PF`) and `copy_user_pml4()`. Promote
  sole-owner pages in place and reclaim user PML4 trees via
  `vmm_destroy_user_pml4()`.
- Add `SYS_PROC_WAIT = 28` and `SYS_VM_PROTECT = 29` alongside `SYS_PROC_CREATE`
  and `SYS_VM_*`. Expose `<sys/proc.h>`/`<sys/vm.h>` and POSIX
  `<sys/mman.h>`/`<sys/wait.h>`. Reject `PROT_WRITE | PROT_EXEC` with `-EACCES`.

## Rejected alternatives

- Putting a static 2 MiB `uint16_t` refcount array in kernel `.bss`.
- Eagerly copying pages in `VMCloneCow` instead of resolving real `#PF` faults.

## Consequences

- `proctest` and `procchild` verify `ProcCreate`, `waitpid` (`WNOHANG` and exit
  status 42), `mmap`/`mprotect`/`msync`/`munmap`, W^X rejection, and Ring 3 COW
  faults in headless QEMU.
```
