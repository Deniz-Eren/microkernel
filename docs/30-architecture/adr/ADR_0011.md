# ADR_0011 — COW fork, bootfs execve, Kernel FD Table, and Ring 3 pipeserv

```{adr} COW fork, bootfs execve, kernel FD table, and Ring 3 pipeserv
:id: ADR_0011
:status: accepted

## Context

For `SWR_PROC_0004` and `SWR_IPC_0004`, the system must give POSIX.1-2024 Base
`fork`, `execve`, `pipe2`, `dup3`, `fcntl`, and `ppoll` with atomic `O_CLOEXEC`
and signal mask rules. Pipe buffers and `ppoll` queues in Ring 0 do not obey
`ADR_0003`.

## Decision

- The kernel adds syscalls `42` through `47`: `SYS_PROC_FORK` (address-space
  `vmm_clone_user_cow` with `FS.base` and FPU inheritance), `SYS_PROC_EXECVE`
  (bootfs ELF load with `argv` and `envp`, `O_CLOEXEC` close, and standard
  signal handlers), `SYS_PROC_GETPID`, `SYS_PROC_GETPPID`, `SYS_FD_OP`, and
  `SYS_SIGPENDING`.
- `pcb_t.fds[]` keeps only compact descriptor metadata (`struct __k_fd_entry`)
  for each process. The kernel sends `PULSE_CODE_FD_CLOSE` pulses on `execve`
  `O_CLOEXEC` close and process exit.
- Ring 3 `servers/pipeserv` (`/dev/pipe`) operates pipe ring buffers, reader and
  writer refcounts, deferred synchronous I/O, and timeout `ppoll` queues through
  IPC.
- `fork`, `execve`, `pipe2`, `dup3`, `fcntl`, `close`, `poll`, `ppoll`, and
  `sigpending` in `libc` stay async-signal-safe with no heap allocation.

## Rejected alternatives

- Pipe ring buffers and `ppoll` wait queues in Ring 0.
- Non-atomic `fcntl` calls for `O_CLOEXEC` after `pipe` or `dup2`.

## Results

- `forkpipetest` and `exechelper` do tests of COW `fork`, `execve`, and atomic
  `O_CLOEXEC` in headless QEMU. They also do tests of Ring 3 pipes, `POLLHUP`,
  and `ppoll` `sigmask` `EINTR`.
- `SWR_PROC_0004` and `SWR_IPC_0004` are active, and `posix.headers` and
  `posix.async_signal_safe` do checks on them.
```
